Who I am

I'm Foysal Ahammad, a developer who mostly builds for Android and AI agents. I'm not the smartest person in the room — I'm just the one who keeps trying until the thing actually works.

My main project, Agent 24, is an autonomous AI coding and technical assistant for Android: a full terminal, a code editor, 31 built-in tools, 13 model providers and a permission system — all running locally on the phone. I built it because I wanted it, and because I had no idea how hard it would be.
